Stream English Study Materials

I know that, for many students, my YouTube videos are not enough. The highest grades are not gained purely by listening to someone else's ideas - you need the opportunity to practice, to revise and to read model essays. That's why I make these resources. Here you'll find a range of materials all designed to get support and enhance your skills for GCSE English Language and Literature.

I also provide resources for teachers. These resources aim to help you maximise your time with high-quality materials to suit high-ability students. I'm also aware that teachers use my YouTube video as homework assignments; lots of the resources aim to help your students get the most out of that homework.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which exam boards do your resources cover?

Most resources are built around the AQA and Edexcel IGCSE English Language and Literature specifications. Because they teach transferable exam skills — language analysis, structure and essay writing — students on other boards such as OCR and Eduqas will find much of the content useful too.

Are these study materials suitable for independent revision?

Yes. Every guide is written to be used on its own for self-study, with clear explanations, worked examples and model answers. They also pair naturally with our free video lessons if you'd like to see a topic explained step by step.

Do you cover both English Language and English Literature?

We do. You'll find Paper 1 and Paper 2 preparation for English Language alongside set-text study guides, quotation banks and essay plans for English Literature. Use the category filters above to browse by subject.

How do I access a resource after I buy it?

Digital resources are available to download straight after checkout, and the download link is also saved to your account so you can return to it at any time.
